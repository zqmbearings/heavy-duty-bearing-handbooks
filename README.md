## 2. Four Critical Large-Bore Field Anti-Error Rules

1. **1:30 (`K30`) vs. 1:12 (`K`) Tapered Bore Guardrail:** Wide-series `240` and `241` spherical roller bearings (e.g., [24072 CA/W33](https://www.zqmbearings.com/products/24072-ca-w33/)) use **1:30 (`K30`)** tapered bores only. Standard `222/223/230/231/232` series use **1:12 (`K`)**. Cross-mounting causes edge contact and inner-ring cracking.
2. **ISO 104 Spherical Roller Thrust Bearings (`293/294 EM`):** Models such as [29488 EM](https://www.zqmbearings.com/products/29488-em/) (`440 × 780 × 206 mm`, `410 kg`) feature cylindrical straight bores (no `K` taper, no `W33` groove) and require continuous oil-bath or circulating oil lubrication with rigid shoulder support (`da min = 595 mm`, `Da max = 670 mm`).
3. **Rolling Mill `-0.5 mm` Reduced-OD Locating Bearings (`QJF / 116xxx`):** Axial locating four-point contact ball bearings like [QJF1058MB / 116756](https://www.zqmbearings.com/products/116756/) (`280 × 419.5 × 65 mm`) intentionally carry a `-0.5 mm` outer diameter so radial rolling forces bypass the locating bearing. Never substitute standard `420 mm` `QJ` bearings.
4. **Four-Row Rolling Mill Assemblies (`FC/FCD/FCDP` & `TQO`):** Verify under-roller envelope diameter `Fw` on cylindrical sets ([FCDP5678275/YA3](https://www.zqmbearings.com/products/fcdp-5678275-ya3/), [FC 6084218/YA3](https://www.zqmbearings.com/products/fc6084218-ya3/)) and keep factory-matched ISO 492 overall width `T` spacer sets paired on four-row tapered assemblies ([LM277149DA/LM277110/LM277110D](https://www.zqmbearings.com/products/lm277149da-lm277110-lm277110d/)).
